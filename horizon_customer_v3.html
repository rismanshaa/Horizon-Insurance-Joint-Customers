❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Horizon — answered simply.

Who can be insured under one policy?
+
Horizon covers two people under a single joint policy. This includes married couples, domestic partners, parent and child, or any two individuals with an insurable interest. Both insured must be between 25–40 years old at the time of enrolment.
When do I start receiving retirement income?
+
Retirement annuity payments begin automatically at age 60 (for the primary insured) and continue for 20 years. You don't need to do anything — it's set up from the start of your policy.
What happens if one of us passes away during the premium-paying period?
+
The benefit depends on when it happens:

• Years 1–10: all premiums paid are returned with 4.5% annual interest.
• Years 11–15: 50% of the Sum Assured is paid immediately to the survivor or heirs.
• Year 16 onwards: the full 100% Sum Assured is paid immediately.

The surviving insured may choose to continue or close the policy.
What happens to the annuity if one of us passes during retirement?
+
If one insured passes during retirement: 50% lump sum of the Sum Assured is paid, and the annuity continues at 50% for the surviving partner. If both pass, the 50% annuity continues to heirs until the 10-year guarantee period ends.
Do I need a medical exam to enrol?
+
For a Sum Assured of up to Rp 800 Million, you only need to fill in a health questionnaire — no clinic visit required. For Rp 1 Billion or Rp 1.5 Billion, a full medical examination is required before the policy is issued.
Can we separate the joint policy later?
+
Yes! Horizon includes a Split Policy Feature. If a qualifying life event occurs (divorce, change of guardian, or adoption), the joint policy can be split into two independent individual policies — with no new medical exam — within 12 months of the event. A one-time admin fee of Rp 500,000 applies.
Are the riders mandatory, or can I choose?
+
All riders are optional add-ons. You can add any combination of: TPD Rider, Critical Illness Rider (15 diseases), COLA Rider (+3.5%/yr inflation adjustment), Accident Rider (+25% SA), and Premium Waiver. Adding riders increases your monthly premium — our consultants can help you pick what fits your needs and budget.
What if I want to cancel or surrender the policy early?
+
Early surrender is possible. The surrender value grows over time: 0% in years 1–4, rising to 15% at year 5, 35% by year 7, 50% by year 9, and 55–60% at year 10 and beyond. We always recommend speaking with a consultant before surrendering, as staying invested delivers significantly more long-term value.
How is the premium amount determined?
+
Your premium depends on three factors: the entry ages of both insured lives, the Sum Assured you choose, and any riders you add. Generally, the younger you enrol, the lower your premium.
